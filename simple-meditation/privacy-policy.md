# Privacy Policy for Simple Meditation

**Last updated:** December 7, 2024

## Overview

Simple Meditation is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains our data practices.

## Data Collection

Simple Meditation **does not collect, store, or share** any personal information or user data.

## Local Storage

All meditation audio files and app preferences are stored locally on your device only.

## Network Usage

The app uses an internet connection solely to:
- Download meditation content from our server on first launch
- No personal data is transmitted during this process

## Third-Party Services

Simple Meditation does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
